## 3. Format & rules

### Tournament format

Pick the structure that fits your numbers and the time you have:

| Format | How it works | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single elimination (knockout) | Lose once and you are out. Fast and simple. | Large fields / limited time |
| Double elimination | Players get a second chance via a losers' bracket. | Fairer, but takes longer |
| Round robin | Everyone plays everyone in a group; most wins advances. | Small fields; guarantees everyone several games |
| Groups + knockout | Round-robin groups, then the top players go to a knockout. | 12+ players — everyone gets games and it still has a clear final |

**Suggested default:** a group stage (groups of 3–4) followed by a single-elimination knockout for the top two from each group. It balances "everyone gets to play" with a manageable schedule.

### Match format

* Each match is best of 3 games (the final can be best of 5).
* Games are played to 11 points, win by 2.
* Service alternates every 2 points (every point once it reaches 10–10).
* Players switch ends after each game.

### Categories

* **Singles** — decide whether to run one open category or split it.
* **Doubles** — optional, and popular for mixed-ability fun.
* Consider separate **beginner** and **advanced** brackets if you have a wide range of skill levels, so it stays fun for everyone.

### Seeding & tie-breakers

* Seed known strong players so they do not meet in round one (or draw randomly for a purely social vibe).
* Group tie-breakers: head-to-head result first, then points difference, then total points won.

## 10. Code of conduct & safety

Keep it friendly: good sportsmanship, respect the calls, no heckling. Make sure the playing area has enough clear space so no one trips or collides, and keep walkways clear of bags.

## 11. Communication plan

* **Announcement:** kick-off message with the date, format, and sign-up link.
* **Reminders:** one when registration is closing, and one the day before.
* **Live updates:** post results and next-round fixtures during the event.
* **Wrap-up:** winners, photos, and a thank-you.
